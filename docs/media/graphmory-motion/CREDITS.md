# Graphics and sound credits

Graphics: original Graphmory conceptual artwork and animation, MIT, included here. The third-party logos below retain their owners’ rights and are excluded from that MIT grant.

Two quiet effects are copied from the brag skill's bundled Kenney library: `interface/click_003.ogg` and `impact/impactSoft_medium_000.ogg`. The skill identifies this library as CC0 (public domain), from [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets), via [brag](https://github.com/latent-spaces/brag). Effects are attenuated and timed at 7.7 and 17.5 seconds. No music or narration is included.

Fonts are loaded from the rendering machine; system font files are not redistributed. This Mac render uses Helvetica and Times. To reproduce elsewhere, pass installed local fonts with `--font-sans` and `--font-serif`.
